Rules

  • Two hard sessions a week, max, counting both sports. Everything else is easy: runs below 143 bpm, rides below 75% FTP.
  • Swaps. Easy run → easy ride at 1.5–2× the time. Sunday's second run → 2–3 h ride (except W24). Quality run → bike version: freely in the base, after that at most once every three weeks.
  • ★ sessions don't get swapped. A missed session is dropped, not stacked.
  • Morning HRV check. Balanced: as planned. One low night or RHR +3: lower end of the reps. LOW status, RHR +5 or sleep under 70: quality becomes easy. The day after a night out is always easy.
  • Fuel 60–90 g carbs/h on runs over 90′ and rides over 2 h. From W25, rehearse race fuelling at 90–100 g/h.

Right-glute rules

  1. Right side first, always. One extra set on the right for the bridge and hip extension exercises.
  2. Wake it up with what works. The bridge + knee opening is the one you can feel; do it before the harder ones.
  3. Hand on the right glute, 2″ squeeze at the top. Hamstring or lower back taking over? Shorten the range, tuck the pelvis under, bring your feet closer to your backside in bridges.
  4. End the set when you lose the right glute. Good reps over a full count.

Mobility routine after training, morning or evening

Good mornings · lat stretch · lying lumbar rotation · prone press-up · cat-camel · deep lunge with rotation · kneeling back stretch (hands on a chair) · hamstring/adductor stretch · hip flexor stretch with rotation · seated hip rotation · alternating hamstring/quad stretch · iliopsoas stretch · half-kneeling thoracic rotation · standing side bend · knee-to-wall ankle stretch, 10/side (new: the screening flagged stiff ankles)

Objectives

  1. Hold the arch. Ankle stops rolling in on landing.
  2. Spread and control the toes. Little toe out, away from the bunionette.
  3. Push off over the big toe, not the outside of the foot. Less load on the 5th metatarsal head.
  4. Ankle range. Knee-to-wall ≥ 10 cm both sides.
  5. Calf and Achilles capacity for 2,500 m of descent.

Rules

  1. Barefoot, on a hard floor.
  2. Shoes with a wide toe box, daily and running. The bunionettes and the 5th metatarsal pain respond to room more than to exercise.
  3. 5th metatarsal pain above 3/10, or worse the next morning: drop that exercise for a week. Pain that stays: podiatrist for a metatarsal pad or insole.
  4. Daily: 10 short-foot holds while brushing your teeth.
  5. Quality over count. Toes curl or the ankle rolls in: end the set.
  6. Feet pointing outward comes from the hip. The glute work in A–C covers it; here, keep the knee over the 2nd toe.
