# Source plans

The Godefroy 75 plan is built on these documents: SWAP Running plans (David & Megan Roche) and two physio programs. This file summarises each one so later changes can be checked against them.

The PDFs themselves are **not** in this repo (it is public and Cloudflare serves the repo root). Full PDFs and plain-text extracts live in the claude.ai project files under `sources/` (`sources/text/*.txt` for search).

SWAP plans are written in miles; 1 mi ≈ 1.6 km. Ranges are "several plans in one": low end = intermediate, high end = very advanced. This plan uses the low end.

## Shared SWAP principles

- Mon rest (heat like sauna/hot tub "ideal"). Fri very easy or x-train before the weekend.
- Two quality days: a Tue/Wed workout and a structured Saturday long run. Everything else easy (Z1–Z2, by effort; it's ok for HR to rise on climbs).
- Hill strides (20–30″ at 4–8%, jog down) and flat strides (5×20″ fast/2′ easy, ~800 m–mile effort, not sprints) on most easy days.
- Workouts by effort: 1-hour effort (≈ threshold), 10k/5k/3k/mile effort, "steady" (≈ 50k), M effort.
- Workout pattern: VO2/speed, then steady or moderate running on tired legs to "shuttle lactate".
- Uphill treadmill: threshold reps at 6–8% (5′ reps, 2′ easy), Z2 aerobic at 10–15%. Outdoors, halve the interval duration (e.g. 6–10×2.5′ hills).
- Long runs "easy/mod": run with intention, Z2→Z3 on climbs, high-carb fuelling (75+ g/h). Tempo early in the long run "makes it play longer"; hills or a harder segment near the end for fatigue resistance.
- Downhills: "strong downs" for eccentric durability; deliberately get sore in some late-block long runs.
- Doubles always optional (20–45′ easy, uphill TM or x-train).
- Health first: when in doubt, cut intensity before volume; sub x-train or rest before pushing through pain. Don't force Sunday runs.
- Strength: Mountain Legs after easy runs; "Full Strength" (squats, back extensions, lunges, step-ups) ~2×/week, not on the rest day or the day before a workout/long run. Dropped in the final 1–2 weeks.
- Core Snack routine 1–2× daily (in the ultra plans).

## Running plans

### Champion Plan 50k–50 mile (12 wk) — basis for the trail block (W19–30)
- Tue: hill strides or the main workout; Wed: workout or easy/x-train (alternates). Thu: 75–120′ easy x-train or uphill TM Z2 (75–105′ at 10–15%).
- Workouts rotate: hill reps (6×2′ 5k effort + 2 mi steady), 16×1′/1′, 120/60/30 ladders, uphill TM threshold 6–12×5′ at 8%, 600s/800s on the track, 4–6×5′ at 1-hour effort + steady.
- Saturday long runs build 12–16 → 20–25 mi (W8), with 5×45″ hills near the end, strong downs, a "Power Hour" (W7) and a race-simulation (W9: 10–15 mi moderate to mod/hard).
- Sunday 8–20 mi easy, often with hills at the end; x-train always allowed.
- Taper W11–12: 6–10×2′ at 1-hour effort, then 3–4×5′ + 5×30″ hills race week.
- Notes: think about the race's vert profile from W5; weight-vest hiking if the race is hike-heavy; sub-ultra training races allowed with an extra rest day.

### Champion Plan 100 km–100 mile (16 wk) — used for Dodentocht 100k
- Same weekly structure, Thu x-train with intervals in the first weeks. Big uphill TM sessions at 8–10% (12–15×3′, 8–12×4′, 8–12×5′, 2–4×10′).
- W10 Big Day (marathon/50k/50 mi race or moderate 50k, "get very sore"), W11 mid-cycle rest block. Long runs up to 20–25 mi; no mid-week strength from W13.
- Stef's feedback: too few hard sessions late in the block, got boring.

### Keep-It-Simple 100k–100 mile (12 wk)
- No treadmill assumed. One Tue workout + structured Saturday long run on trails.
- Workouts progress 12–16×1′/1′ → 3′ hills → 2′/1′ → 5′/6′ at 1-hour effort → 20–40′ mod/hard over hills.
- Long runs 8–16 → 25 mi–50k (W9, or a 50k race), "Just Say No To Rhabdo" downhill runs, "be a downhill monster".

### 100 Mile Breakthrough (16 wk)
- Near-copy of the 100k–100 mile Champion plan, plus heat-suit doubles and more steady running.

### 100 Mile Race Intermediate/Advanced (12 wk)
- Lower volume (18–110 mi/week). One Wed workout, Sat long run on trails, lots of hiking. Peak 25–30 mi (W9) with a 40–60′ tempo on trails.

### 200 Mile Plan (16 wk)
- One Tue workout, Sat long run with tempo, Sunday 4–10 h hikes. 50k moderate 5 weeks out. Speed Legs / Mountain Legs alternate.

### Sub-Ultra Mountain/Trail (half marathon–marathon, 8 wk)
- Tue: workout (90″ hills, 16×1′/1′, ladders, 2′/2′ into 10′ moderate, 3′ hills at 8–10% + 15′ moderate). Sat long run on race-like trails with a tempo or late hard segment.
- Race week: 4×90″ hills + 10′ at 1-hour effort. In the race, around threshold on climbs is ok.

### Champion Half Marathon → Marathon (8 + 4 wk) — reference for the 16k block
- "Zone compression": first 8 weeks compress VO2 and LT2 (make near-VO2 pace sustainable for 1 h), then bring LT2 towards M pace.
- Tue workout + Thu uphill TM threshold (60–105′ total, 8–15×3′ or 5–10×5′ at 6%; outside, half the reps) + Sat long run with threshold pieces (1 mi at 1-hour effort with fast starts/finishes, float recoveries).
- Most athletes should take the x-train option on Thursday. Strength 2×/week, not the day before workouts or long runs.
- HM race week: Tue 4–5×3′ at 1-hour effort + 4–5×30″ at 3k; Sat 3–6 mi with 4×20″ and 1′ slightly faster than HM pace.

### 5k/10k Speed (8 wk) — reference for the 16k block
- Tue workout (4×(4×1′/1′), 3/2/1 ladders, 30/60/90 pyramids, 6×2′ hills + 5×45″, 3×(3×90″), 6×5′ at 1-hour effort), Sat easy/mod long run with threshold (6′ reps, 20′ tempo, late 6′ hard).
- W5 and W7 have a second midweek workout (Thu). Race-week opener: 4×2′ at 10k + 4×1′ at 3k.
- Race pacing: smooth first third, strong second, fast final third.

### 12-Week Advanced Marathon
- Tue strides/hills, Wed workout (16×1′/1′ + M effort, 5×3′ hills + 15′ M on tired legs, 800s, 5k time trial, 6–8×5′), Sat long run with M-effort blocks (up to 20–24 mi with 5/4/3/2/1 mi at M).
- Strength: Mountain Legs + 2×10 squats + 2×20 back extensions; none in W4 and W8 (before big weeks) or the last two weeks.

### 8-Week Road Marathon (Int/Adv/Pro)
- Wed workout, Sat long run with M-effort segments. 10k time trial in W6 to set the M goal. "M effort" by feel, not pace, until the final weeks.

### Long-Term Base (10 wk + bonus week)
- For aerobic development between races. Weeks 5–10 can be repeated; insert the bonus week every 4–6 weeks.
- Wed alternates uphill TM threshold (3–6 → 6–12×5′, then 3–6×10′ at 1-hour effort) with easy/moderate steady runs. Sat 6–20 mi easy/mod over hills.
- "When in doubt, default to easy. Intensity is far more stress than volume."

## Cross-training and strength

### Bike Training Plan for Runners (6 wk)
- For injured runners; every day on the bike (or elliptical). Tue/Wed/Sat structured, rest easy.
- Sessions: 8×30″ hard, 4×(4×1′/1′), 1-2-3-4-3-2-1, 30/30s, 2–3×10′ at 1-hour effort, 20′ hard (FTP ≈ 95% of 20′ power), 30′ steady, 10/8/6/4/2′.
- Cadence 80–100; out-of-the-saddle pushes; 60+ g carbs/h on rides over 45′ (90+ on hard days). Cap indoor rides at ~2 h.

### SWAP Strength Work Cheat Sheet
- Before training: band work (side steps, monster walks), lunge matrix, leg swings, partial wake-up legs.
- After training: activation (rear/side lunges, leg swings), light stretching optional.
- Mountain Legs (Wed, Sat): 20–50 single-leg rear lunges, 30–100 step-ups. Speed Legs (Thu, Sun): rear lunges, Bulgarian split squats, single-leg deadlifts, goblet squats, step-ups; weight can go up as you build.
- Myrtl condensed (clams, lateral leg raises, donkey kicks, hurdle forward/back) most days. Upper body/core: push-ups/pull-ups, Russian twists, bicycle crunches.
- Self-care: foam rolling or percussive massage daily.
